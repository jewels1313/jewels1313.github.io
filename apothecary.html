Lets start creating your first potion!

Heres a list of the ingredients you will require:
  • mint
  • rosemary
  • dried roses
  • jasime
  • mushrooms
Now follow these steps to make a healing potion! Make sure not to miss a single step, or else...
  1. prepare all the ingredients in front of you
  2. start by crushing up the mint and rosemary leaves
  3. then crush the dried roses as well
  4. add them together and mix
  5. then mix in essential oils with the mushrooms and swirl it around to create a paste
  6. now boil some water and make a jasmine tea
  7. finally, add all the ingrredients to the jasmine tea and mix well
